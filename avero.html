How Avero Works

A simple loop for awareness, verification, and rewards.

📚
01 · Learn

Engage with campaign content

Users complete micro-learning journeys, awareness content, guided surveys, or quiz-based educational modules designed around a sponsor’s campaign objective.

🛡️
02 · Verify

Validate participation quality

Avero checks completion quality using quizzes, timing rules, attention checks, trust scoring, and anti-fraud logic before counting a response as valid.

🎁
03 · Reward

Reward users, report outcomes

Once participation is verified, users receive digital rewards while sponsors get transparent campaign analytics, completion metrics, and engagement insights.

Two-Sided Platform

Trust Layer

Blockchain-backed transparency, without turning the product into crypto UX.

Avero uses blockchain selectively as a trust and accounting layer—for reward records, sponsor auditability, and campaign integrity—while keeping the user experience simple, mainstream, and mobile-first.

Reward accounting
Transparent records of sponsor-funded reward allocation and reward distribution events.
Campaign auditability
Campaign rules, versions, reward issuance, and completion proofs can be logged for stronger sponsor trust.
Participant trust signals
Support long-term quality scoring and verified completion history without forcing wallets on end users.
